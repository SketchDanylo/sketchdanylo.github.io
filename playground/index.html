      ABOUT THIS LAB

      A model, with limits.

      The sandbox uses an experimental reactive force field with fitted molecular examples. Its reactions and rates are qualitative.

      Physical clock
      Each step advances 1 fs. At 1×, the target is 20,000 steps/s: 20 ps per real second. At 0.1×, one step takes one second. × is a playback setting.
      Why a mixture may seem inactive
      A second at the target sandbox rate covers only 20 picoseconds. A reaction may need activation, solvent, or chemistry this model cannot represent. For stationary imports, use Resample thermal motion.
      What is checked
      H₂ 0.741 Å / 436 kJ/mol, water 104.2°, the water dimer at −18 kJ/mol, 2 H₂ + O₂ → 2 H₂O at −486 (lit. −484), H + H₂ barrier 43 (lit. 40). Na + Cl₂ → NaCl runs at 300 K; CH₄ + O₂ at 3500 K passes through CH₂O and OH·.
      Known to be wrong
      Spin is absent (O₂ is patched to act as the triplet diradical it is). Cl + H₂ and OH + H₂ have barriers that are too high, H + O₂ → OH + O too low. CO gets a double bond instead of a triple. No tunnelling, excited states or solvent.
      Three dimensions
      A top-down orthographic view of a soft-walled 3D slab. Zoom changes the view, never atom sizes or the chamber volume.
      Energy & pressure
      Force checks hold internal bond orders fixed. Changing those heuristic orders can cause energy drift; extreme-collision speed clamps also remove energy. The wall reservoir exchanges heat near the boundaries. Its temperature approaches the setpoint over a specified simulation time, while collisions and interactions carry heat through the sample. Wall pressure is averaged normal stress, with no pressure controller.
      What you see
      The field contours show tabulated atomic sizes through Gaussian surfaces. They are not a quantum electron-density calculation.
      Zero kelvin
      A classical geometry minimum with zero initial velocities. Quantum zero-point motion is not represented.

      Methods: MD integration ↗ Validated reactive force fields ↗. This engine is not ReaxFF.
